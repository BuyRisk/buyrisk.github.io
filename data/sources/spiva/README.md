# `spiva/` — S&P Dow Jones Indices SPIVA® Scorecard

**Source:** S&P Dow Jones Indices, *SPIVA U.S. Year-End 2025 Scorecard* —
<https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/research-insights/spiva/>
**Terms:** Copyrighted report. The raw PDF is **git-ignored**; only the specific
extracted figures (with attribution) ship.
**Citation:** S&P Dow Jones Indices, *SPIVA U.S. Scorecard, Year-End 2025*.

## Why it's here (future active-vs-passive tool)

The canonical evidence on **how often actively managed funds beat their
benchmark** — the share underperforming over 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, and 20 years, by
category. The empirical backbone for the *Can You Beat the Market?* tool's claim
that persistent outperformance is rare.

## Extraction

The report ships as a PDF; the key percentages live in its tables (e.g., "% of
funds underperforming the S&P 500" over each horizon). These will be transcribed
into a small generated `.ts` — with attribution — when the tool is built. No raw
report content is redistributed.
